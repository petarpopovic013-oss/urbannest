# Urban Nest landing page topology

Source: `docs/design-references/inspo.webp`. No live URL exists, so all dimensions below are screenshot-derived targets rather than computed DOM values.

1. Header — light background, inline navigation, dark rounded call CTA.
2. Hero — centered editorial headline and supporting copy, followed by a full-bleed architectural image.
3. Brand Promise — dark section, centered light logo and statement, then a three-image strip.
4. Owner Outcomes — dark section with a centered title and four proof-style columns, using process outcomes instead of unsupported KPIs.
5. Full Management — dark asymmetric two-column composition with a circular image mask and service list.
6. Transparency — light section with stacked circular imagery and owner-reporting copy.
7. Belgrade Market — dark section with market copy, three operating principles, and a decorative Belgrade line-map visual.
8. Final CTA — large architectural image with an overlapping dark curved panel and phone CTA.
9. Footer — dark navigation footer with oversized decorative brand mark.

Page behavior is standard vertical scrolling. No tabs, carousels, parallax, or server data are required.
